A box that opens a calendar, a clock or both, to choose a date and a time.

## What it is for
- Birthdays, appointments, deadlines, a time of day.

## How it works
- Picks decides whether it chooses a date, a time or both.
- Date is written year-month-day, such as 2026-10-03, and Time is hour:minute, such as 14:30. Leave one empty for none chosen.
- 24-Hour Clock shows 14:30 instead of 2:30 PM. Minute Step limits the minutes that can be chosen, such as to every 15.
- On Change is the name of a method that runs when the date or time changes.

## Tips
- Placeholder is what the box says while nothing is chosen.
