Two panes, one above the other, with a bar between them that the player can drag.

## What it is for
- A document with a console under it, a map over a list: any two areas where the player decides how much room each gets.

## How it holds things
- It holds up to two elements: the first is the top pane and the second is the bottom one. It sets their sizes, so their own widths and heights are not used.
- Divider Position is how much of the height the top pane gets, from 0 to 1.

## Tips
- Smallest Pane stops the divider being dragged until a pane disappears. Largest First Pane limits the top one.
- For two panes side by side, use Split Left and Right.
