A layout that puts its items on top of each other, in the same place.

## What it is for
- A picture with a label over it, a background with the content on top, a badge in the corner of a button.

## How it works
- Every item is placed within the layout by the layout's Alignment: the middle by default, or a corner or an edge.
- Items drawn later are on top, so the order in the hierarchy is the order from the back to the front.
- The items keep their own sizes.

## Tips
- To put an item in a corner, set the layout's Alignment to that corner. All items share it, so use more than one stack to put items in different corners.
