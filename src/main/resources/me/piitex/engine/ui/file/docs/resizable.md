A container the player can drag the corners of to make bigger or smaller.

## What it is for
- Windows and panels inside a game that the player arranges: an inventory, a chat box, a tool palette.

## Tips
- User Can Resize turns the dragging on or off. Show Handles draws the grips.
- Scale Contents makes what is inside grow and shrink with it. Otherwise the contents keep their size and the container just shows more or less of them.
- The minimum and maximum sizes stop the player making it too small or too big. A maximum of 0 means no limit.
