Two panes side by side, with a bar between them that the player can drag.

## What it is for
- A list on the left and the details on the right, a menu beside a preview: any two areas where the player decides how much room each gets.

## How it holds things
- It holds up to two elements: the first is the left pane and the second is the right one. It sets their sizes, so their own widths and heights are not used.
- Divider Position is how much of the width the left pane gets, from 0 to 1.

## Tips
- Smallest Pane stops the divider being dragged until a pane disappears. Largest First Pane limits the left one.
- For one pane above the other, use Split Top and Bottom.
